eProcurement in Funding & Tenders Portal
Tender Opportunities | 30 October 2026

New process for submission of deliverables

If your specific/direct contract was signed on or after 26 October 2026, you should use the new Deliverables section in the F&T Portal to upload and submit deliverables. If the contracting authority has already defined a list of deliverables, you can use the predefined deliverables placeholders; if not, you can upload your deliverables by clicking on Create new deliverable in the same section.

Deliverable Approval Certificate

After the contracting authority approves the deliverable, you will receive a F&T Portal notification and you can consult the Deliverable Approval Certificate in the Portal. You can use this certificate to create an invoice in the F&T Portal (the invoice line will be created automatically) or reference it in PEPPOL invoices.

Time & Means specific contracts using Corporate Time Management

In case of Time & Means specific contracts under FWCs supported by the European Commission’s Corporate Time Management (CTM) solution (see here the list of these FWCs), the submitted monthly reports of the service providers will be automatically transmitted for approval to the contracting authority. Contractors will receive the Deliverable Approval Certificates in the F&T Portal and can use them to create and submit invoices.

For all other Time & Means specific or direct contracts the monthly reports should be submitted through the F&T Portal following the new process for submission of deliverables.

Documentation and support

More details are available in the F&T Portal release note and the F&T Portal eProcurement wiki

If you need support, contact EC-FUNDING-TENDER-SERVICE-DESK@ec.europa.eu
